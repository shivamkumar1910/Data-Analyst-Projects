# Power BI Dashboard Guide

## Overview

This dashboard is designed for a beginner-level retail sales analysis project. It should feel clean, readable, and focused on decision-making.

## Page 1: Executive Overview

### Title
Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

### KPI Cards
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value

### Visuals
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Region

### Slicers
- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

### Design Notes
- Use a simple dark blue / teal / gray palette
- Keep KPI cards aligned across the top
- Use line charts for time trends and bar charts for category/region comparison

## Page 2: Product and Category Analysis

### Visuals
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Sub-Category
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit

### Slicers
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Region

### Design Notes
- Use one chart per section with consistent sizes
- Highlight high-performing and low-performing categories
- Keep product charts limited to top/bottom lists to avoid clutter

## Page 3: Customer and Regional Analysis

### Visuals
- Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Top 10 Customers
- Sales by Segment
- Profit by Segment
- Monthly Sales Trend

### Slicers
- Region
- Segment
- Year

### Design Notes
- Use bar charts for customer and regional comparisons
- Use trend lines for monthly performance
- Keep the page readable and uncluttered

## Recommended Color Palette

- Primary blue: #2E86C1
- Green: #2ECC71
- Orange: #F39C12
- Red: #E74C3C
- Gray: #5D6D7E
- Background: #F8F9F9

## Dashboard Tips

- Keep the layout clean and professional
- Add clear titles to each visual
- Use consistent axes and labels
- Avoid overly crowded visuals
- Use slicers only where they help answer business questions

## Final Goal

The dashboard should help a manager quickly understand sales performance, регион profitability, customer trends, and category performance without needing to explore raw data manually.
