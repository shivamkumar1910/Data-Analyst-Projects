# Business Insights

This file is intentionally kept ready for actual analysis once the Sample Superstore dataset is added to the project.

## Sales Performance

- Findings will be added after the cleaned dataset is loaded and analyzed.
- Relevant metrics will include total sales, average sales, month-over-month trends, and category/regional contributions.

## Profitability

- Findings will be added after profit totals and margins are calculated.
- Key focus: where profit is strongest and where it is weakest.

## Regional Performance

- Findings will be added after regional sales and profit totals are reviewed.
- Key focus: which regions drive revenue and which show margin pressure.

## Category Performance

- Findings will be added after sales and profit analysis by category and sub-category.
- Key focus: which categories should receive investment or review.

## Product Performance

- Findings will be added after ranking products by sales and profit.
- Key focus: top-performing products and low-profit products.

## Customer Performance

- Findings will be added after customer-level sales and profit analysis.
- Key focus: top customers and repeat-value segments.

## Discount Analysis

- Findings will be added after discount averages and profit relationships are reviewed.
- Key focus: whether heavy discounting is hurting profitability.

## Important Note

No business findings are included here without analyzing the actual dataset. Once the CSV is placed in the project and the scripts are run, this file can be updated with evidence-based insights and recommendations.
